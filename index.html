Market Alchemy

Probabilistic Structural Decision System
Structure does not decide.
It redistributes probability.

Market Alchemy describes how outcomes emerge under constraint.

It is not a deterministic system.
It is a probability-shaping structure where certain paths become more likely as constraints increase.

When constraints are low, systems explore many trajectories.
When constraints increase, the distribution of outcomes narrows.

Core Principle

Systems under pressure do not select a single outcome.
They shift probability mass across possible futures.

As structural constraint increases:
- some paths lose probability
- others gain dominance
- one trajectory may become statistically dominant (not guaranteed)

Structural Compression

Compression reduces accessible state space (Ω reduction). It does not remove alternatives, but suppresses their probability.

What appears as “decision” is often a statistical convergence effect.

Containment (Structural Depth)

Containment defines probability boundaries, not absolute outcomes. It shapes what becomes likely, not what becomes possible.

Structural depth increases when probability distribution becomes more constrained.

Market Behavior

Markets do not execute deterministic paths.
They continuously reallocate probability under liquidity and constraint pressure.

Price is the observable trace of this redistribution process.

Vector Formation

A vector is not a guaranteed direction.
It is a direction with increasing probability persistence after structural filtering.

Non-reversion indicates probability dominance, not certainty.
