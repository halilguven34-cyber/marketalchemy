Market Alchemy (MA) is a behavioral control-support architecture
designed for a biologically constrained human agent
to recognize the friction created by their own impulses,
within a market system governed by physical laws,
making the cost of staying at the table visible,
and assisting capital to remain only in low-friction hands.

MA is not a signal service.
MA is not an automated profit system.
MA is not a promise mechanism.

  • Vector / Friction
  • Time = cost
  • Reform = delay
  • Eliminative logic

Market Alchemy
does not always produce content.
Does not answer every question.
Does not accept every demand.

Silence
is a natural part of this architecture.
