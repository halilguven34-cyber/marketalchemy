Abstract

This work proposes a structural interpretation of time based on hierarchical containment. Local temporal measurement is associated with configuration volume, while global temporal ordering is defined through structural depth in a containment graph. Phase transitions correspond to containment expansions which generate irreversible organizational layers. Within this framework, time is interpreted not as a flowing parameter but as the measurable depth of hierarchical containment.
