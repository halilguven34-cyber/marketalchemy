Creator of Market Alchemy

Halil İbrahim Güven is the creator and system architect of Market Alchemy Research, an independent research program investigating structural determination, structural depth, complex systems, time, markets, and computational methods.

The program develops theoretical frameworks, formal models, empirical tests, computational experiments, reproducible research tools, and working decision-system architectures.

MarketAlchemy.io is the public research and technology interface of this program.

Research Identity

His work combines a background in financial and technical analysis with independent research in structural systems and computational methods. He has worked as a financial analyst for more than 18 years, including experience in banking analysis, and has pursued independent research and authorship since 2016.

His research program has developed from theoretical work on Structural Depth and structural determination toward formalization, computational implementation, empirical testing, and reproducibility.

Core Research Frameworks

Structural Determination Axioms (SDA)

An axiomatic layer defining a minimal structural vocabulary for investigating systems before selecting a particular representation or measurement.

Structural Depth Theory (SDT)

A framework for studying how systems become organized through layers of constraints, relations, accessibility, and containment.

Empirical Computational Research

The research program translates selected theoretical structures into computable quantities, experiments, live telemetry, matched controls, and reproducible computational procedures.

From Research to Technology

The Market Alchemy architecture connects theoretical research with computational implementation. Research outputs can become models, experiments, decision-support engines, observability systems, and reproducible computational tools.

The public Observatory provides an accessible runtime layer through which selected computational outputs can be observed in real time.
